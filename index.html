Why a Critical Care RN for AI Training?

Healthcare AI is only as good as the clinical expertise behind its training data.

AI models trained on medical data need human reviewers who can catch what algorithms miss. A model might generate an answer that looks correct on paper but would raise a red flag for any experienced clinician. That's where I come in. I know what a real clinical assessment looks like because I've done thousands of them.

I've worked across Epic, Metavision, and Cerner EMR platforms, so I understand how clinical documentation is structured and where gaps tend to appear. I know the difference between a textbook answer and what happens at the bedside. That kind of judgment is hard to teach, and it's exactly what RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) processes depend on.

My writing background means I can also produce high-quality prompt responses, evaluate the quality of AI-generated medical text, and provide detailed rationale for my annotations. I don't just flag something as wrong. I explain why it's wrong, what the correct clinical reasoning should be, and how the output could mislead a clinician or harm a patient.
